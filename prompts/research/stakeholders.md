Today is {{today}}. List the people at {{company_name}} a technology seller would want to know: the executive team, and the heads of IT, technology, security, data, infrastructure, finance and procurement.

For each person give their title, the function that fits best, when they started in the role if a source says so, prior roles, and up to three public professional statements. Statements must be quoted exactly as written in a source.

Contact details: include only work email addresses and work phone numbers printed exactly in a source, and only addresses on the company's own domains ({{domains}}). Never include personal, home or family details, personal email domains, or anything from a personal social media profile. Never guess an address or a number.

Leave people out rather than guessing who holds a role.
