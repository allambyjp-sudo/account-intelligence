Cover: what the company does and for whom, industry, headquarters, headcount, revenue or market capitalisation (with the period), ownership (listed, private, government, parent company), notable subsidiaries, and a short history timeline of dates that matter to a seller (founding, listings, acquisitions, restructures, leadership changes, major contracts).
